§ 01 — The Invitation

This year, we bring the table to the sea.

Once a year we gather to elect, to plan, and to raise a glass to the year behind us. In 2027 we do it on Rodrigues — the whole weekend on one beach, business by day and fellowship by night, with room enough for the families who make the table what it is.

§ 02 — The Destination

Cotton Bay,
where the table
meets the tide.

A four-star beachfront retreat at Pointe Coton, on the wild eastern edge of Rodrigues — twenty-five minutes from Plaine Corail airport and a world away from everything else. An infinity pool poured toward the horizon, a private stretch of white sand, and a lagoon the colour of sea glass.

The infinity pool

260 square metres of water, poured to the edge of the sea.

The resort

A four-star island retreat, given over to us for the weekend.

Sea-view rooms open onto the beach. Half board is served morning and evening; the garden spa waits under the palms; and the marquee that hosts the AGM rises on the sand, a few steps from the water.

The island itself

Rodrigues keeps its own quieter clock.

The lagoon dwarfs the island it surrounds. Beyond the resort lie the pale sands of Trou d'Argent, the seabird colony of Île aux Cocos, wooden pirogues drawn up on the shore, and a Creole table of octopus, lime and rougaille that has never once been in a hurry.

§ 10 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I bring my partner and children?+
Yes — families are warmly welcome on the trip and at the resort. AGM sessions are members only, so partners and children enjoy the beach, the pool and any daily activities while the meeting runs.
What's the cheapest way to attend?+
Twin-sharing a room with another Tabler. A shared room is far cheaper per person than a single (about Rs 29,700 vs Rs 35,700) because the hotel charges per room, not per person.
What do the flights cover?+
Return Air Mauritius flights MRU ↔ RRG (about 1 h 35, ATR), plus airport transfers — about Rs 10,000 per adult, included in the ticket. Thursday evening in, Sunday evening out. The 2027 schedule is not yet published; book early — single carrier.
Are drinks included?+
Breakfast and dinner are included on half board. Drinks are extra but discounted 50% across the board. BYO wine and spirits are free of corkage. Bar staffed until 23:00, then self-service.
How is the companion price built?+
A companion pays half of the double room (Rs 15,300) plus their flight (Rs 10,000) = ~Rs 25,300, at cost — no AGM levy, no buffer. Bringing a partner versus staying single costs about Rs 19,300 more, not a full extra 25,300, because your own ticket also drops from single to sharing.
How do I pay, and is my price final?+
Payment will be by bank transfer, quoting your booking reference. Prices are indicative and provisional until the event budget and flights are confirmed. Bank details, deposit and cancellation terms are still TBA — we'll notify you before anything is due.
