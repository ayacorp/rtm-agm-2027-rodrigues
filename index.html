Why Rodrigues

The National AGM, off the main island.

Round Table Mauritius is gathering for the 2027 National AGM on Rodrigues — the smaller, slower island in the Mauritian family, about 600 km to the east.

This site is the public booking and information page for Tablers and partners. It follows the shape of other Round Table meeting sites: dates first, then programme, travel, stay, and a clear route to register when the official package is published.

Travel & logistics

Mauritius, then a short hop east.

Public aviation facts only. Group flights, meeting points and baggage rules for the AGM remain TBA.

01 · Mainland

Arrive in Mauritius

International arrivals use Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU). Leave connection time before the domestic Rodrigues sector — how much is a personal / published schedule decision, not an AGM promise.

02 · Domestic

MRU → RRG

Rodrigues is served via Sir Gaëtan Duval Airport (RRG / Plaine Corail). Air Mauritius operates the nonstop sector. Typical block time is about 1 hour 35 minutes over roughly 600 km. Book your own seats unless a group allotment is later published.

03 · On island

Port Mathurin & beyond

Port Mathurin is the capital. Airport transfers, car hire and any official shuttle are unpublished. Photographs of the harbour illustrate the island — they are not a nominated meeting point.

Check live Air Mauritius timetables closer to May 2027. This page does not sell flights.

Financials explained

What we can say before the numbers exist.

FAQ-style only. No invented package fees, bank accounts, IBAN, SWIFT, or refund percentages.

Why is there a package at all?

A national AGM weekend typically pools shared costs — meeting space, some meals, programme elements and on-island logistics — so Tablers book one package instead of many invoices. The exact split for 2027 will come from the official financials brief.

When will prices be published?

When Round Table Mauritius releases the booking sheet. This website will then replace every TBA price with those figures only. Until that file is in the repo, treat any number you see elsewhere as unofficial.

What currency will I pay in?

Mauritius uses the Mauritian rupee (MUR). Whether the AGM invoice is MUR, another currency, or a mix is TBA. Do not convert a guessed figure.

Are flights and rooms included?

Unknown until the booking sheet says so. Many Round Table meetings sell a ticket without hotel (see other associations’ HYM sites). Others bundle nights. Rodrigues 2027 has not confirmed either model here.

How do I pay? Who do I pay?

Only through the official Round Table Mauritius AGM channel, once published. This site will not list account names, numbers or mobile-money handles until they appear in the signed financials document.
