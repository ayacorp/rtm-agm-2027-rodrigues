## 4. Ticket pricing (revenue model)

Model: **expenses per head → add a small buffer → gross ticket sold to attendees.** Break-even is the priority; the buffer becomes a modest surplus if numbers hold, or absorbs overruns. Illustrative below uses **Rs 3,000/member event levy + 5% buffer** — swap the levy for actuals once known.

| Ticket | Build-up | Gross price |
|---|---|---|
| **Member — sharing** | (15,300 + 10,000 + 3,000) × 1.05 | **≈ Rs 29,700** |
| **Member — single** | (21,000 + 10,000 + 3,000) × 1.05 | **≈ Rs 35,700** |
| **Companion adult** (no AGM) | 15,300 + 10,000, at cost | **≈ Rs 25,300** |
| **Child** (shares room) | 4,200 + flight | **Rs 4,200 + flight (TBC)** |

At ~120 members, a 5% buffer ≈ **Rs 1,400/head → ~Rs 170,000 cushion** — enough to soak up a catering or marquee overrun, or land a small surplus.

**Paid optional add-ons (priced separately, not in the ticket):** spa treatments (10–30% off, TBC) · tours & activities · traditional fishing trip · lunch (if not bundled) · extra drinks (all at −50%). **Possibly free:** hotel daily activities for companions / kids.

## 5. Written summary

Round Table Mauritius can hold its **2027 AGM at Cotton Bay Resort & Spa, Rodrigues, over three nights, 13–16 May 2027.** The AGM itself runs **outdoors under a marquee for ~100–150 members**; spouses, partners and children join the trip but not the sessions, and the resort's 88 rooms house up to ~176 adults.

The commercial model is built **expenses-first**: each member's hard cost is **~Rs 25,300 sharing** (room + half board Rs 15,300 + flights and transport Rs 10,000), or ~Rs 31,000 in a single room. Adding a modest event levy and a 5% buffer puts an **indicative member ticket at ~Rs 29,700 sharing** — priced to cover costs with a small cushion rather than to profit. Companions pay ~Rs 25,300 at cost; children are room-rate plus flight. Drinks are −50% across the board with BYO wine and spirits free, and spa, tours and the fishing trip sit outside the ticket as paid add-ons.

The one missing number is the **event budget** (marquee, AGM catering, gala, alcohol) — once that lands and we have a rough member/companion split, the ticket price firms up and the booking sheet goes live.
