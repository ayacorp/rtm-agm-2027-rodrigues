# Claude Design brief — RTM AGM 2027 website · Cotton Bay, Rodrigues

## How to read this
This is **context, content and ideas — not a spec.** You own every design and structure decision. Take the directions that resonate, improve or drop the rest, and surprise us if you see something stronger. The only things that must stay exactly as written are the **facts and prices** (marked 🔒) — those are real and can't drift. Everything aesthetic is yours.

## The event 🔒 (keep accurate)
Round Table Mauritius — a men's service organisation (members 18–40), motto **"Adopt · Adapt · Improve"** — holds its 2027 Annual General Meeting at **Cotton Bay Resort & Spa, Pointe Coton, Rodrigues**, **Thursday 13 – Sunday 16 May 2027 (3 nights)**. Members ("Tablers") and their families book and pay to attend. Members meet under a beachfront marquee (members-only); partners and children enjoy the island. The site's job: get people to book and pay. Contact: **secretary.rtm@gmail.com**.

## The feeling we're chasing (a direction, not a rule)
A $10k+ luxury-resort / high-end-event site: cinematic, editorial, deep — an engraved invitation opened on a beach. Round Table heritage meeting island luxury; ceremonial but warm. If you read a stronger mood, follow it.

## Brand ideas (starting point — refine freely)
- Palette we lean toward: deep Round Table navy `#0B2A5B`, heraldic gold `#C9A24B` (used sparingly, like foil), ivory / parchment `#FBF7EE`, and a whisper of Rodrigues ocean-teal `#0E6E7D`. Shift it if a better harmony serves the photography.
- Type direction: a high-contrast display serif (Fraunces / Cormorant Garamond) with a clean sans (Manrope / Inter). Your call.
- A round-table **seal / crest** motif (motto around the rim, a stylised table inside) as logo, subtle hero element and faint watermark. Please design an **original** crest — 🔒 don't reproduce Round Table International's official logo.

## Imagery ideas
Real photography of the actual place should carry the site: Cotton Bay's 260 m² infinity pool over the sea, the private white-sand beach, sea-view rooms, the Kono-Kono restaurant, the garden spa, golden-hour aerials — and Rodrigues itself: the turquoise lagoon, Trou d'Argent, Île aux Cocos, traditional fishing pirogues, island aerials, Creole culture and food. Source whatever tells the story best.

## Our own media — please leave slots
We'll drop in our own photos and videos, so elegant, clearly-labelled placeholders (framed and captioned, not grey boxes) would help: a hero video slot, a Gallery / Memories set, a past-AGM quote, and an optional gala highlight video.

## Content to cover (order and grouping are yours — one page or several)
Hero · invitation ("bring the table to the sea") · the destination (Cotton Bay + Rodrigues) · what's included (3 nights + half board · return flights + transfers · AGM under the marquee · welcome + gala dinners · all drinks −50% · resort & beach) · the weekend (Thu arrive → Fri AGM → Sat gala → Sun late checkout & fly home) · booking calculator · gallery / our media · how to pay (bank transfer + reference) · reserve form · optional extras (spa, tours, traditional fishing trip, lunches, extra drinks, possibly free daily activities) · FAQ · footer.

Group these into one long scrolling page or several pages — whatever converts and impresses most. Only ask: keep the booking flow close to the hero.

## The calculator (numbers 🔒, presentation yours)
Per person, 3 nights, half board, drinks excluded. Show a small "indicative — provisional" note.
- Inputs: your room (twin-share with a Tabler / double with my partner / single) and children joining (0–2; rooms sleep 2 adults + 2 kids).
- Prices: Member sharing **Rs 29,700** · Member single **Rs 35,700** · Companion / partner **Rs 25,300** · Child **Rs 10,200**.
- Output: a live line-item breakdown, total, one-line room summary, and a reference like `RTM27-<SURNAME>-<4 digits>`.

*(Build detail, if useful: accommodation Rs 15,300 sharing / Rs 21,000 single per adult; flight Rs 10,000 / adult; AGM share Rs 3,000 / member; 5% buffer; child Rs 4,200 room + ~Rs 6,000 flight — all provisional.)*

## Motion (ideas — keep it tasteful)
Cinematic hero reveal, gentle parallax on imagery, scroll-triggered fades, a slowly rotating seal, count-ups in the calculator, a condensing sticky nav, hover micro-interactions. Spend the boldness on one or two signature moments. 🔒 Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, mobile-first, visible keyboard focus.

## Copy tone
Warm, a little ceremonial, never corporate — "Adopt · Adapt · Improve." Errors and empty states should guide, not just fail.

## What must stay true 🔒
The event facts and dates · the four prices and the calculator maths · members-only AGM · an original crest (not RTI's logo) · accessibility (reduced-motion, focus, mobile).

## Yours to decide
Structure (single- vs multi-page), layout, exact palette and type within the direction, section order and grouping, animation choices, imagery selection — and any better idea you bring.

## Reference files (attached)
- `rtm-agm-2027-rodrigues.html` — the approved static version. Lift the copy and calculator logic from it; restructure and re-skin the design however you like.
- The customer **booking sheet** — clean copy and the four prices in readable form.
